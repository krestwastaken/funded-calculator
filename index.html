Worked example — crossing the line

Splits are calculated on current profit, so a single request can straddle both tiers. Say a trader on a $50,000 account has $6,000 in net profit and requests $3,000.

The first $2,000 sits above the $4,000 line and pays at 80/20 — $1,600 to the trader. The remaining $1,000 falls below the line and pays at 50/50 — $500 to the trader. Total payout: $2,100, not $2,400.

$2,000 × 0.80 = $1,600  +  $1,000 × 0.50 = $500  =  $2,100 received
Worked example — consistency and trading days

On a $50,000 Fast Pass Funded Sim account, say a trader logs five trading days: $180, $210, $95, $260, and $190 — $935 in total profit. Four of those days clear the $150 daily minimum; the $95 day doesn't count, so only 4 of 5 qualifying days are logged.

Their best day, $260, is 27.8% of the $935 total — comfortably under the 45% consistency cap. The split itself isn't the blocker here: the missing fifth qualifying day is.

$260 ÷ $935 = 27.8% consistency  ·  4 of 5 qualifying days logged
Path to Funded Live
$3,000 gross profit$10,000 milestone

Quick Pay accounts are reviewed for Funded Live at $10,000 gross profit — tracked separately on each funded account. Profit above that threshold isn't carried forward: once an account hits $10,000, it's reviewed for promotion and any excess is removed. Holding several Quick Pay accounts doesn't pool progress — each clears $10,000 on its own.

0 of 5 payout requests5th request triggers review

Fast Pass runs on an entirely different trigger: review for Funded Live happens at the 5th payout request, not a profit figure. Profit and consistency still determine whether each request goes through — they just don't decide when the Funded Live review happens.

Notes
  • No consistency rule. Quick Pay Funded Sim has no daily profit cap once funded.
  • No minimum trading days. Payouts are available from day one of funding, gated only by the $250 minimum request and current profit.
  • 45% consistency rule. No single trading day may exceed 45% of total Funded Sim profit.
  • 5 profitable days required per payout. Each must clear the account size's daily minimum — $150 (50K), $200 (100K), or $250 (150K).
  • Payout requests are capped per size. $1,500 (50K), $1,850 (100K), or $2,250 (150K) maximum per request.
